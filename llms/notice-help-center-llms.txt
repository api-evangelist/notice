## Data

- [How is Notice Price determined?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8338741-how-is-notice-price-determined.md): Notice Price is determined by a proprietary algorithm incorporating private and public market data, updated every three seconds.
- [How accurate is Notice Price?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8339353-how-accurate-is-notice-price.md): Notice Price accuracy is measured by our ability to predict the price of the next trade.
- [What are Notice Price Drivers?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8092239-what-are-notice-price-drivers.md): Notice Price Drivers is the circle icon shown next to Notice Price.
- [What is Coverage Ratio?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8291326-what-is-coverage-ratio.md): Coverage Ratio measures a company's ability to return capital to common stock shareholders in a M&A exit at the current market cap.
- [What is Activity Score?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8291644-what-is-activity-score.md): Activity Score measures relative liquidity in a company's stock in the private markets
- [What are Notice Rankings?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8339496-what-are-notice-rankings.md): Notice Rankings are ranked lists of private companies based on Notice Price.
- [What are Notice Industries?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8339502-what-are-notice-industries.md): Notice Industries allows you to browse over 6,000 private companies using 100+ industry categories.
- [What are Industry Categories?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8291647-what-are-industry-categories.md): Industry categories segment companies to make the Notice Industries research tool more powerful
- [How are Notice.co 50 Index \(N50\) components selected?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8341942-how-are-notice-co-50-index-n50-components-selected.md): N50 is comprised of the 50 largest private market companies meeting specific criteria.
- [How is the Notice.co 50 Index \(N50\) updated?](https://learn.notice.co/en/articles/8341952-how-is-the-notice-co-50-index-n50-updated.md): N50 is updated in multiple ways so that it remains accurate and representative of the overall private market.
